Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I have my home professionally pressure washed?

Many homes benefit from cleaning every 12–24 months. Coastal exposure, trees and weather can affect the ideal schedule.

Is pressure washing safe for my home's exterior?

Yes, when the cleaning method is matched to the material. Different surfaces require different techniques and pressure levels.

How do you determine the price?

Five Star evaluates the property in person, considering size, surfaces, accessibility and condition before providing recommendations and pricing.

Do I need to be home during service?

In many cases, no, provided the areas being cleaned are accessible. An in-person estimate can be completed beforehand.
